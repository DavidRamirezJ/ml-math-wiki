# Ludwig Otto Hesse (1811–1874)

> **Why they matter to ML:** The matrix of second-order partial derivatives — the Hessian — is named after him; it encodes curvature and powers second-order optimization.

## Life (brief)
German mathematician, born in Königsberg, where he studied under [[carl-gustav-jacob-jacobi|Jacobi]].
He worked mainly in analytic geometry and the theory of algebraic invariants and
curves, holding professorships in Königsberg, Heidelberg, and Munich.

## Key contributions
- **Hessian matrix** — the square matrix of all second-order partial derivatives
  of a scalar function, symmetric for twice-continuously-differentiable functions
  and a measure of local curvature. Introduced in his work on algebraic curves;
  maps to [[hessian]].

## Connections
- Concepts: [[hessian]], [[taylor-series]] (second-order term), [[partial-derivative]]
- Related work: Newton's method (Ch. 7), Laplace approximation (Ch. 6); studied under [[carl-gustav-jacob-jacobi]]
