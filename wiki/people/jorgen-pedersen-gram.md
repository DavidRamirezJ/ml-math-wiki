# Jørgen Pedersen Gram (1850–1916)

> **Why they matter to ML:** The Gram–Schmidt process and the Gram matrix bear his name — orthonormalization and the kernel/covariance matrices central to ML.

## Life (brief)
Danish mathematician and actuary. Much of his mathematical work grew out of practical problems in
insurance and forestry. He also worked in number theory and series expansions, and died in a
bicycle accident in Copenhagen.

## Key contributions
- **Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization** (with Schmidt): a constructive method to turn any basis into an
  orthonormal one. Maps to [[gram-schmidt-orthogonalization]].
- **Gram matrix:** the matrix $A_{ij}=\langle b_i,b_j\rangle$ of pairwise inner products. Its
  positive (semi)definiteness characterizes valid [[inner-product|inner products]] and
  [[symmetric-positive-definite-matrix|SPD]] structure, and it is exactly the kernel matrix used in
  SVMs and Gaussian processes.
