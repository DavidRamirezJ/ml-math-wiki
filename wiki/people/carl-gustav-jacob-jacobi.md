# Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi (1804–1851)

> **Why they matter to ML:** The matrix of first-order partial derivatives — the Jacobian — is named for him, and it is the central object for differentiating vector-valued maps and rescaling probability densities.

## Life (brief)
German mathematician, born in Potsdam, professor at Königsberg. One of the most
prolific mathematicians of the 19th century, he made foundational contributions
to elliptic functions, partial differential equations, dynamics (the
Hamilton–Jacobi equation), and the theory of determinants.

## Key contributions
- **Jacobian matrix / Jacobian determinant** — the matrix of all first-order
  partial derivatives of a vector-valued function, and its determinant as the
  local volume-scaling factor under a change of variables. Maps to [[jacobian]].
  His systematic study of functional determinants ("De determinantibus
  functionalibus", 1841) is why the determinant of this matrix carries his name.
