# Emanuel Parzen (1929–2016)

> **Why they matter to ML:** He formalized kernel density estimation — the "Parzen window" — a foundational nonparametric method for estimating a distribution directly from data.

## Life (brief)
Emanuel Parzen was an American statistician, a pioneer of time-series analysis and nonparametric density
estimation, with faculty positions at Stanford and Texas A&M University.

## Key contributions
- **Kernel density estimation (Parzen, 1962).** Estimating a density by placing a kernel on every data
  point and averaging, $p(x)=\tfrac{1}{Nh}\sum_n k\!\big(\tfrac{x-x_n}{h}\big)$ — proposed independently by
  Murray Rosenblatt (1956), hence "Rosenblatt–Parzen." Often called the **Parzen-window** estimator. Maps
  to → [[kernel-density-estimation]].
