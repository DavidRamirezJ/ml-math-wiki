# Joseph-Louis Lagrange (1736–1813)

> **Why they matter to ML:** His multipliers are the standard tool for optimizing under constraints — the foundation of SVM duals and constrained ML objectives.

## Life (brief)
Italian-born French mathematician and astronomer (born Giuseppe Lodovico Lagrangia in
Turin). A leading figure of 18th-century analysis and mechanics, he succeeded Euler at
the Berlin Academy and later worked in Paris, authoring the landmark *Mécanique
analytique*. He helped design the metric system.

## Key contributions
- **Lagrange multipliers / the Lagrangian.** A method to convert a constrained
  minimization into an unconstrained one by adding a weighted ("priced") penalty per
  constraint. Today the core of constrained optimization and **Lagrangian duality**. Maps
  to [[lagrange-multipliers]], and underlies the duals in [[linear-programming]] and
  [[quadratic-programming]].
- **Calculus of variations & analytical mechanics.** The Euler–Lagrange equations
  reformulate mechanics in terms of an action functional — the same variational thinking
  behind modern optimization.
