# Carl Eckart (1902–1973)

> **Why they matter to ML:** with Gale Young he proved that the truncated SVD is the optimal low-rank approximation of a matrix — the theorem behind PCA, compression, and dimensionality reduction.

## Life (brief)
American physicist and oceanographer. He worked in quantum mechanics, geophysics, and acoustics, and
held senior posts at the Scripps Institution of Oceanography. Among mathematicians he is best
remembered for a 1936 result on matrix approximation, joint with the mathematician Gale Young.

## Key contributions
- **The Eckart–Young theorem (1936):** the best rank-$k$ approximation of a matrix (in spectral and
  Frobenius norm) is obtained by truncating its [[singular-value-decomposition]] to the top $k$
  singular triples, with approximation error $\sigma_{k+1}$ (Thm 4.25). Maps to
  [[low-rank-approximation]].
