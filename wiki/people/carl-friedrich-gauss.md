# Carl Friedrich Gauss (1777–1855)

> **Why they matter to ML:** his name is on two pillars of the field — **Gaussian elimination** (solving linear systems) and the **Gaussian distribution** (the bell curve at the heart of probabilistic ML).

## Life (brief)
German mathematician, often called the "Prince of Mathematicians." Worked across number theory,
algebra, statistics, geodesy, astronomy, and physics. A foundational figure whose tools are still
the default in scientific computing and machine learning.

## Key contributions
- **Gaussian elimination** — the systematic row-reduction algorithm for solving a
  [[system-of-linear-equations]]. → [[gaussian-elimination]] *(stub)*
- **Gaussian (normal) distribution** — the bell curve; the default noise/likelihood model in ML.
  → [[gaussian-distribution]] *(stub, MML Ch. 6)*
- **Least squares** — the method behind [[linear-regression]] when a system has no exact solution
  (MML Ch. 9). *(attribution shared historically with Legendre)*
