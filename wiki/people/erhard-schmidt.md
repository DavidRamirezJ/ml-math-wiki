# Erhard Schmidt (1876–1959)

> **Why they matter to ML:** Co-creator of Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization and a founder of Hilbert-space theory — the infinite-dimensional inner-product spaces behind kernel methods.

## Life (brief)
German mathematician, a student of David Hilbert. He helped found modern functional analysis and
the theory of Hilbert spaces, and was a central figure in early 20th-century mathematics in Berlin.

## Key contributions
- **Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization:** gave the explicit iterative formulation (1907) that turns a
  basis into an [[orthonormal-basis|orthonormal basis]]. Maps to [[gram-schmidt-orthogonalization]].
- **Hilbert space theory:** formalized complete inner-product spaces, the rigorous home of the
  [[inner-product|inner product of functions]] (Eq 3.37) and the foundation of kernel methods and
  Gaussian processes in ML.
